I opened Acme Studio four years ago with two wheels and a kiln I bought secondhand. This winter we have eight wheels, and I'd love to see you at one of them.

Our six-week wheel class is for complete beginners. You'll learn to centre clay, throw a cylinder and turn it into a mug or a bowl. Then you glaze it, and we fire it here.

Groups are kept to eight, so there's always someone at your elbow when the clay starts to wobble. All the clay, tools and firings are included in the price.
